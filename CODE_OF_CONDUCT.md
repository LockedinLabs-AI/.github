# Code of conduct

LockedIn Labs welcomes useful, respectful participation in its open source projects. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Make room for different experience levels and perspectives.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.
- Keep reports and examples free of credentials, customer information, personal data, and protected health information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close a discussion, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful. To report conduct concerns privately, use the [LockedIn Labs contact form](https://lockedinlabs.ai/contact/) to request a private channel; provide only the project and a safe way to reach you in the initial message.
